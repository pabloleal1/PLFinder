QA technology

iQA

A platform originally developed by PLFinder to bring testing into one workspace.

From a project workspace, iQA centralizes web, mobile, API, manual and accessibility testing, local execution, reporting, Jira integration and CI/CD pipeline integration.

It accelerates test creation, reduces repetitive work and improves QA traceability with a clearer view of what is tested, what fails and what is ready before each delivery.
